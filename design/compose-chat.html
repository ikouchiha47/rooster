What changed

  • Chips, not tags. 28–30px tall, name-first, tap ✕ to remove — the current tiny tags become legible targets.
  • The body is a message area, not a form box. Sent text appears as an outgoing bubble; the draft sits under it.
  • Pinned composer. One line + a round send; keyboard-inset like a chat, no fixed form field eating space.
  • Per-recipient delivery reads inline under the bubble (“Anil · delivered”, “Babu · sending…”), so fan-out is visible.
  • Group MMS stays a greyed option in the recipient area until the platform allows it.
  • Everyone gets their own SMS — the hint says so up front, no surprise.
